### Fixed
- The display Mac no longer gets stuck at about three refreshes of delay. With 0.1.9 and 0.1.10 it sometimes took about 23 ms instead of about 7 ms, with the same picture, until it recovered on its own. It now lets two frames wait for the screen instead of one, so a slow screen refresh no longer holds up the next frame.

### Improved
- Develop › Show Statistics adds "Drawn on the display Mac": how many times a second it draws, and how long frames wait before being drawn.

### Note
- Update both Macs: the display Mac for the fix, and the Mac you work on for the new row.
