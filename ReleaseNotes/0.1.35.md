### Changed
- Hold Esc for a second to leave MokaScreen on the MacBook showing the display. A message appears while you hold. A press of Esc still goes to the Mac you work on. ⌘Esc, introduced in 0.1.34, did not work.

### Fixed
- Switching to some apps on the MacBook showing the display, such as Safari, could hide the picture and bring it straight back. One likely cause is fixed; the MacBook now also records why the picture is hidden or shown, in case it is not the only one.
