### Fixed
- When something starts moving after the screen has been still for a second or more, the display Mac no longer pauses for up to a tenth of a second. macOS sometimes never confirms that the first frames after a still moment reached the screen, and MokaScreen waited up to 100 ms for that confirmation before drawing more. It now stops waiting two refreshes after a frame was due.

### Changed
- With Show Statistics on, "Drawn on the display Mac" reads "draws a second", since one draw can show several frames, and calls the time Smooth Motion holds a frame "held" rather than "wait".
