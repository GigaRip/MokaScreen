### New
- The first release of MokaScreen. It turns a second MacBook Pro into an extended display for the first, over a Thunderbolt cable, at the full resolution of its screen and up to 120 Hz.
- Works with 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pros in any combination. The extended display takes the size of the screen that shows it, so every pixel lands on exactly one pixel there.
- Only the parts of the screen that changed are sent, uncompressed. MokaScreen talks only over the Thunderbolt Bridge, never over Wi-Fi.
- Check for Updates in the MokaScreen menu.
