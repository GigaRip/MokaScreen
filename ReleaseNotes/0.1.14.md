### New
- Develop › Smooth Motion (⌥⌘M) makes scrolling and animation on the display Mac evenly spaced. The display Mac holds each frame until a fixed delay after it was captured, instead of showing it the moment it arrives. The delay adjusts to how much frame arrival times vary, and is never more than one refresh (8.3 ms). Turn it on or off from either Mac; both follow.
- Develop › Show Statistics adds "Uneven frames": how many frames a second reached the display Mac's screen sooner or later than their spacing when captured, and Smooth Motion's current delay.
