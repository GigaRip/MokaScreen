### Changed
- The first part of the delay in the statistics is now called Cable. It is timed from the moment macOS hands MokaScreen a frame until the frame is on the cable, so it no longer reads 0.0 ms.

### Experimental
- MokaScreen asks macOS for frames without a minimum gap between them, to see whether scrolling reaches a full 120 frames a second.
- Larger network buffers on both Macs, to see whether large frames cross the cable faster.
