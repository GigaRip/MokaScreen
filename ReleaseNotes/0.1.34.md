### New
- Share the keyboard and trackpad (in Settings, off by default). While the other MacBook shows your display, its keyboard and trackpad also control the Mac you work on: typing, the pointer, clicks, drags and two-finger scrolling. On that MacBook, ⌘Tab still switches its own apps. The Mac you work on asks for Accessibility permission the first time. This is new and still being tested; pinch, rotate and Force click are not sent.

### Changed
- ⌘Esc now leaves MokaScreen on the MacBook showing the display. Plain Esc no longer does, so that it can be used as an ordinary key.
