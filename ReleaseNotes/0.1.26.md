### New
- Develop › Smooth Motion Minimum Delay adds 25 ms and 30 ms, for comparing with the default 20 ms. A longer minimum leaves more room for the slowest frames, so the schedule shifts less often, at the cost of that much more delay, including for the pointer.

### Changed
- Smooth Motion can now hold a frame for up to 30 ms instead of 25 ms, and the display Mac keeps eight frames' worth of receive buffers instead of six, so a 30 ms minimum never runs out of room. This uses about 60 MB more memory on the display Mac.
