### Improved
- All the statistics are now in one place: the window on the Mac you work on. With Develop › Show Statistics (⌥⌘S) on either Mac, it shows the whole delay split into its two parts, capture and the cable on this Mac and the display Mac's share, with a minute of history.
- The last-minute chart keeps the newest moment at its right edge.

### Fixed
- Statistics no longer slow down the picture. The panels drawn over the screen in 0.1.6 and 0.1.7 added about a refresh of delay on the display Mac, so they are gone.
