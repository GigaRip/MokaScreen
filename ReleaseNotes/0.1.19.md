### Changed
- Smooth Motion keeps scrolling even when frames take uneven times to cross the cable. A scroll changes most of the screen in every frame, and those large frames arrive up to 15 ms early or late. Smooth Motion now holds a frame for up to three refreshes (25 ms) instead of one, so frames reach the screen spaced as they were captured. The extra delay applies only while things are moving; it falls back within about a second after motion calms, and resets at once after a pause.
- With Smooth Motion on, the delay in Show Statistics is higher while things move, and "Drawn on the display Mac" counts the time a frame is held as waiting.
- Develop › Record Frame Timing records captures and the display Mac's timing over the same 10 seconds.
