### New
- Statistics panels on both Macs. Press ⌥⌘S on either Mac to show them on both: the Mac you work on shows the whole trip from capture to the other screen, and the display Mac shows its share, each with a minute of history.
- The display Mac's panel appears over the shared screen, so its numbers are visible while it's showing your display.

### Important
- Update MokaScreen on both Macs. Version 0.1.6 can't connect to earlier versions.
