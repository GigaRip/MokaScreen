### New
- Switching to another app on the display Mac pauses MokaScreen. The other Mac's menu bar and windows are no longer drawn under the display Mac's own. Switch back to MokaScreen and the picture returns at once, with no reconnect. Esc still stops.

### Changed
- With Show Statistics on, the latency panel appears once a frame has reached the display Mac, rather than showing "— ms" over an empty chart.
- The Thunderbolt addresses now sit under "Thunderbolt", and the "Bridge interface" row is gone. The interface name is already part of the IPv6 address.

### Note
- MokaScreen now requires macOS 27.2 or later.
- Update both Macs.
