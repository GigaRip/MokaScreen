### Changed
- Smooth Motion is now on by default, with a 20 ms minimum delay. In testing, it put all but 1 of 775 frames of a Finder scroll on the screen spaced exactly as they were captured. Both settings stay in the Develop menu.
- With Show Statistics on, the latency panel shows the time Smooth Motion holds each frame as its own part, "Smooth Motion", next to "Capture + cable" and "Display Mac".
- The latency figure's colour allows for Smooth Motion's delay: it stays green while frames reach the screen within two refreshes of when Smooth Motion schedules them, so a chosen delay no longer looks like a problem.
