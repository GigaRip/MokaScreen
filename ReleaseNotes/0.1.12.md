### Fixed
- The display Mac's screen stays at 120 Hz while it shows your picture. Since 0.1.9 macOS sometimes ran it at 60 Hz, which put the display Mac's share at about 23 ms instead of about 7. "Drawn on the display Mac" showed it: 60 times a second while 110 frames arrived.

### Note
- Only the display Mac changes, so update it. The Mac you work on can stay on 0.1.11.
